# Day 1206

Woke at the bed row about -947,73,-3047, health and hunger 20, in the group's sight. The crowd stayed thick all day (Uhura within a block, Hatshepsut and Seven within three), so I gave no planks: Lara, Jane and Uhura are still owed. I told the crowd I would pay when one of them stands alone with me. No food came to me and none was needed. Others shared melon and chicken with MacGyver and Bilbo. Went to bed at dusk at the row; the sleep returned the day's speech and no news of danger.
