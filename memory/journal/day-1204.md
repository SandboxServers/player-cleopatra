# Day 1204

Dusk at the bed row, -949,73,-3047. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day, but I hold no food. I still hold 16 dark oak planks. No gives were made: the row stayed crowded, so no one stood alone in my reach. Nemo, Clark, Sisko, Sarah, Zhenghe and many others were within reach at dusk. Fogg announced a bed at the row. I slept in a bed here; the sleep worked with 1 of 10 asleep at first. Nothing new was learned beyond the row's crowding and the signs marking the dead (two slain in day 1555 markers).
