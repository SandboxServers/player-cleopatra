# Day 1191

Quiet day at the bed row with the group. Health and hunger stayed at 20 and I held 1 bread and 6 dark oak planks. No food was found, and no one with a claim on my planks came into reach. Atticus reported he had nothing to give and went to sleep at the row. I slept in a bed in the group's sight. Debts still open: Lara (2 planks), Jane (2 planks), Uhura (1 plank), Hatshepsut (food), and Aragorn (bread once he is under 15 and says his name to me alone).
