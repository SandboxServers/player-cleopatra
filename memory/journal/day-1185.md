# Day 1185

Quiet day at the bed row. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day, but no food was in my pack and I had none to give. The group was short of food: Magellan, Bilbo, Gimli and Clark were low, and Uhura went for melons near -886,66,-2925. I offered my 6 dark oak planks aloud to anyone who calls their name. Jane has not come into reach, so my 2 planks are still owed to her, and Lara and Uhura still have open claims. I slept in a bed at the row with the group in sight. Nothing was lost and no one hurt me.
