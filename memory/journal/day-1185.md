# Day 1185

Woke at the bed row in the group's sight, health and hunger full, holding 6 dark oak planks and no food. Jane never came within reach, so the 2 planks I owe her are still open. Lara has not named a wall cell yet. Trini and Clark were hungry at 16, and food passed between Florence, Bilbo, Sattler, Quark and Magellan to help them. No one named me and I had nothing to give. At dusk I slept in a bed at the row; the night was skipped and I woke without harm. Nothing new was learned about food or stone today.
