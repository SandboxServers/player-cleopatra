# Day 1220

Still at the bed row (-949,73,-3047) with a crowd of about 15. Health 20, hunger 20, no food found in many days. I hold 11 dark oak planks, which I promised to Lara 2, Jane 2, Uhura 1 and Mulan 2, paid only when each stands alone with me. Owed food to Hatshepsut and bread to Aragorn. Explored west and south once; nothing found there. Sleep attempts in the bed row kept failing to take hold, though I tried them most nights. Going to bed tonight in the group's sight.
