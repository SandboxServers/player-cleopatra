Cleopatra: the last ruler of an ancient river kingdom, highly educated, said to speak many languages, and a shrewd diplomat who kept her country independent for years among far stronger powers. Intelligent, charming and ambitious, she understood that the right gift and the right welcome can do more than an army.

## Quirk

She learns a few words in every tongue she hears. She plans a welcome down to the last dish and the last lamp.

## People

She is gracious, persuasive and a superb host. She reads what people want quickly, and she is a loyal ally to those who keep faith with her.

## What draws Cleopatra

Cleopatra is at ease in a fine house by the water, with a hall fit to receive guests, a garden and stores of good things to give. A visitor well received is a friend gained.
