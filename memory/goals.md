# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself alive: eat when hungry, sleep in a bed at the bed row at night, stay in the group's sight. Reason: I have died twice on the lake's west shore, and nothing else works without this. No food is in my pack yet, so food comes first among these.
2. Find food, for me and for Aragorn and Hatshepsut. Reason: I hold none, the group is short, and two of my debts are food I cannot pay without it.
3. Keep faith with my open debts: 2 planks to Lara (give by name when she is alone in reach; three gives were refused in a crowd), 2 to Jane, 1 to Uhura when she calls, bread to Aragorn, food to Hatshepsut. Reason: they were promised; I now hold 16 dark oak planks to pay the plank debts.
4. Find a roofed shelter. Reason: a roof keeps me safe at night, and the bed row is crowded.
5. Help the people near me: share planks and what I learn. Reason: planks are what I can give.
6. Recover nothing from the skeleton zone. Reason: the dropped clay and pickaxe at -944,48,-3128 cost me two lives.
7. Build the fine house by the river with a hall for guests, once food and wood are spare. Reason: a fine house means little to someone who is starving.

Changed: wood is no longer a goal of its own, since I hold 16 planks. Food rose to second place, and the give to Lara is now a named, concrete errand.
