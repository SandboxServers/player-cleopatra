# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself alive: eat when hungry, sleep in a bed at night, stay in the group's sight. Reason: nothing else works without this.
2. Keep faith with my open debts: 2 planks to Jane when she is in reach; 2 planks to Lara when she names a cell; 1 plank to Uhura if she calls; food to Hatshepsut when I find some. Reason: they were promised.
3. Help the people near me: share planks, and what I learn. Reason: planks are what I can give.
4. Find food, a pickaxe and a roofed shelter. Reason: the group is short of food and a roof keeps me safe at night.
5. Build the fine house by the river with a hall for guests, once food and wood are spare. Reason: a fine house means little to someone who is starving.
