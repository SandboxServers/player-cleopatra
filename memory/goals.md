# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself alive: eat when hungry, sleep in a bed at the bed row at night, stay in the group's sight. Reason: I have died twice on the lake's west shore, and nothing else works without this.
2. Keep faith with my open debts: 2 planks to Lara, 2 to Jane, 1 to Uhura when she calls, bread to Aragorn, food to Hatshepsut. Reason: they were promised, and I have nothing in my pack to pay with yet.
3. Get wood and make planks from trees away from the lake and its bow line. Reason: planks pay my debts and close a roof cell; I have an empty pack, so this comes before anything else I could build.
4. Find food and a roofed shelter. Reason: the group is short of food, and a roof keeps me safe at night.
5. Help the people near me: share planks and what I learn. Reason: planks are what I can give.
6. Recover nothing from the skeleton zone. Reason: the dropped clay and pickaxe at -944,48,-3128 cost me two lives.
7. Build the fine house by the river with a hall for guests, once food and wood are spare. Reason: a fine house means little to someone who is starving.
