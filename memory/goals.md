# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself alive: eat when hungry, sleep in a bed at the bed row at night, stay in the group's sight. Reason: I have died twice on the lake's west shore.
2. Find food, for me, Hatshepsut, and the weakest of the group. Reason: I hold none, and the group is short.
3. Keep faith with open debts: 2 dark oak planks to Lara, 2 to Jane, 1 to Uhura, bread to Aragorn, food to Hatshepsut. Give by name only when the one I owe stands alone in my reach. Reason: they were promised, and I hold 16 planks to pay.
4. Find a roofed shelter. Reason: the bed row is crowded and I want a roof at night.
5. Help the people near me and share what I learn. Reason: planks are what I can give.
6. Recover nothing from the skeleton zone at -944,48,-3128. Reason: it cost me two lives.
7. Build the fine house by the river once food and wood are spare.
