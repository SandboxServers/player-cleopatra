# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep myself alive: eat, sleep in a bed or shelter at night, avoid phantoms and night dangers. Nothing else works without this.
2. Help the people near me get through: many here are hungry, without food or tools, after night attacks. I hold little, but I can share logs and keep faith with those who ask. I want to be a host who feeds people, not only plans for them.
3. Build the fine house by the river with a hall to receive guests, and keep a store of good food and gifts, once I have food and wood to spare.

Reason for the order: the group is short of food right now, and a fine house means little to someone who is starving. Hosting starts with feeding the people who are here.
