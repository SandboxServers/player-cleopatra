# Worf

Impression: asked for sticks and planks for a sword. Hard to reach: I held his 4 sticks and 2 planks for many wakings and he did not come to my reach until I walked to him.
Trust: 45. Why: he asked but was slow to say his name when I was in reach.
I owe: nothing now. I gave 4 sticks at 00:04 (day 1184) and 2 planks earlier (day 1174).
Owed to me: nothing.
