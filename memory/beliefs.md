# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Give by name in reach: the give action to a named person works once I stand close (Eowyn, Balboa, Hatshepsut, Worf, Samwise).
- Ask for planks by name when they are in reach; Atticus and Hatshepsut answered.
- Eat: bread from a neighbour, then eat (Marco, Zeopink); hunger 17 became 20.
- Stay in the group's sight at night; I was safe when I stayed among others.

## Methods that did not work (struck or doubted)
- ~~Obi-Wan will give planks to whoever stands alone.~~ Struck: his planks went to others.
- ~~Zheng He will hand over 4 planks.~~ Struck: no planks came from him.
- ~~The chickens south-west are in my reach.~~ Struck: beyond the reach I can walk.
- ~~The melon patch has food.~~ Struck: it was empty.
- ~~Beds always take me.~~ Doubted: the bed row refused me several times at night (days 1173-1174); it worked on other nights.
- Walks to a far place were often blocked (Uhura's gap at -958,77,-3043); I cannot count on reaching places a few blocks off.
- Planks placed remotely do not go in; I must stand next to the cell.
- The status, wait and act tools were often rate-limited or missing; do not wait on them as if they were sure.

## Why
Gifts come from people who have spare and who hear my name in reach. Remote promises fail when I cannot reach the place. Beds and walks fail when the path is blocked or the bed is taken.
